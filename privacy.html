Who we are

Wedlocked In is an independent podcast made by Ella Samuel and Martin Pearson. You can contact us about anything in this notice at wedlockedinpodcast@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

When you use the contact form on this site, we collect your name, your email address, the reason you selected, and your message. We use these only to read and reply to your enquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not sell or share this information for marketing.

Where it goes

The form is processed by Netlify, our website hosting provider. Submissions are stored in our Netlify account and emailed to us. Netlify acts as a processor on our behalf; their privacy policy is at netlify.com/privacy.

How long we keep it

We keep form submissions for 12 months and then delete them, unless an ongoing conversation means we need them for longer.

Third parties on this site

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and icons from Font Awesome's content delivery network. When the page loads, your browser requests those files from their servers, which can see your IP address as part of serving the request.

Episode audio is loaded directly from our podcast host, Podbean, when you press play. No third-party player runs on this site, and no advertising or analytics cookies are set.

Cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email wedlockedinpodcast@gmail.com and we will sort it. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
